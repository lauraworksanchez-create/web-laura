A good workshop isn’t a long meeting with sticky notes. It’s a session designed so a team reaches decisions it wouldn’t have made separately.

I prepare every session around the project’s goals: what needs deciding, who needs to be in the room and what information is missing. Then I facilitate, synthesise and deliver an actionable result.

How I work

  1. 01

    Understand

    Prior interviews and a review of what already exists. I arrive knowing where the tensions are.

  2. 02

    Design

    Tailored agenda, exercises and materials, printed or interactive.

  3. 03

    Facilitate

    I run the session so every voice is heard and the conversation moves towards decisions.

  4. 04

    Synthesise

    I turn what came out into insights, priorities and next steps the team can act on.

Sometimes the biggest challenge isn’t not knowing what to do, but not knowing where to start.

Using service design methods, I work with teams to surface what matters, align it and turn it into real decisions.

Because the best ideas are never born alone.

CASE

Positioning workshop for a centenary brand

A food brand with almost a hundred years of history wanted to reach its centenary knowing what to say and to whom. I designed a positioning workshop to draw out strategic insights with the team.

The session combined a word grid to narrow the brand territory, personality axes to place the brand against its competitors, and printed and interactive materials for group work.

What you get

  • A clear map of insights
  • Priorities agreed by the team
  • Next steps with owners
  • The session materials, ready to reuse
