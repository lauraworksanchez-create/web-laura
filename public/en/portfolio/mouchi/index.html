Mouchi

IDENTITY · LOGO · BRANDING

Born as a children’s fashion brand with a nostalgic outlook, a carefully nurtured sensitivity for craftsmanship and a strong commitment to sustainability.

The name, with its emotional meaning and international sound, calls for a visual identity with character and delicacy.

This first phase aims to align the visual universe, aesthetic references and overall graphic tone before moving on to logo proposals.

It evokes the simplicity of a delicate drawing, one that “doesn’t shout, it whispers”, subtly paying tribute to the name Mouchi (owl in Galician) without being literal. The two central arches suggest pages opening up to unfold new stories, reinforcing the idea of growing up unhurried, with each garment as a chapter taking shape.

RESEARCH

Before defining any visual element, I ran a research process with the target audience: mothers of children aged 0 to 6 who care about design, craftsmanship and sustainability. The goal was to understand what they truly value, what frustrates them about current children’s brands and what emotions they wanted a new brand to convey.

Where stories grow

Where less speaks loud

Mouchi isn’t just a logo: it’s a way of expressing who we are and what we want to share with the world. Our visual identity reflects our values, our personality and the way we relate to our community.

This brand book brings together the essential guidelines for using the brand correctly. Its aim is to ensure that every use of the logo, colours and typography keeps Mouchi’s identity coherent and strong, whatever the medium or context.

By following these guidelines, we help Mouchi stay recognisable, consistent and trustworthy at every touchpoint.
