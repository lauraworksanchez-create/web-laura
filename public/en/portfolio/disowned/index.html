DISOWNED

IDENTITY · LOGO · BRANDING

Disowned Factory is a brand with an irreverent, creative attitude, aimed at people looking for products with personality and authentic design.

Its identity is built around originality, transgression and visual play, combining a modern aesthetic with graphic references that connect with young, style-conscious consumers.

The brand is committed to innovation and authenticity, creating pieces that stand out in a saturated market.

The Disowned Factory restyling responds to the need to strengthen its visual identity and adapt it to new opportunities for communication and growth.

Every restyling starts by understanding what must not be touched. Before proposing any change, I diagnosed the existing identity: what was working, what was failing and what the client and their audience expected. The goal was to evolve the visual system without losing the attitude that makes Disowned recognisable.

Keeping the essence

The Disowned Factory restyling responds to the need to strengthen its visual identity and adapt it to new opportunities for communication and growth. The update modernises the graphic elements while keeping the brand’s irreverent essence, improves visual consistency across every application, from packaging to digital, and increases its appeal to its target audience by highlighting its creative, distinctive character.

It also aligns the identity with the brand’s current vision and values, making sure every piece communicates strength, authenticity and style. The result is a stronger, more flexible and memorable identity, ready to position Disowned Factory as a benchmark in its sector.
