EONIA

IDENTITY · LOGO · BRANDING

The word comes from the ancient Greek aion, meaning “age”, which gave Spanish the word “eón”: an indefinite but long period of time, or a major division of geological time.

In astronomy and less formal contexts, an aeon refers to a span of a billion years.

Where you enjoy without asking for permission

Eonia is a brand dedicated to creating unique, memorable experiences, where every detail is designed to connect with guests’ emotions. Its approach combines creativity, style and strategic planning, making sure every event has its own identity and leaves a mark. From concept to execution, Eonia turns ideas into moments that are lived and remembered, adapting to each client’s personality and needs.

The attendee experience

Eonia isn’t just a visual brand. It’s a service that begins long before the music starts and ends days after the event. This journey map captures the attendee’s whole experience, from the first contact with the brand on Instagram to the memory that stays. Each phase has its own touchpoints, emotions and design opportunities, and Eonia’s visual identity has to work in all of them.
