THE HOST EDITION

DIGITAL STRATEGY · UX · SEO · BRAND

Client
The Host Edition, holiday rental and hotel consultancy
Year
2026
Role
Audit, digital strategy, UX, SEO and brand

A consultancy with a beautiful website that wasn’t working for it. The challenge: to be found, understood and easy to contact.

Paula had launched The Host Edition on WordPress.com. Her requests were very specific: review the website, social media templates, more visibility on Google and a site that loads fast.

Before designing anything, I listened to every request and organised them into three blocks, from the urgent to the aspirational. That way every decision had a reason and an order.

Measure first, then move.

I ran an audit with real data: performance with Google PageSpeed Insights across the 7 main pages, technical SEO, the contact journey and the legal framework.

Problems surfaced that you can’t see at first glance: the footer email didn’t work, the Hotels button opened a loose photo and the form rejected ordinary surnames because it required an @. Anyone who wanted to get in touch got lost along the way.

I also reordered the menu so each service sits next to its pricing, published the legally required pages and cookie banner, fixed the sitemap in Search Console and set up the Google Business profile.

From invisible to first choice.

The second phase was the visibility strategy: SEO for Spain, a local blog, Google Business presence and GEO, so that assistants like ChatGPT recommend her when someone asks for a consultancy like hers.

I also set clear expectations: search visibility is a long game, with realistic milestones at months 1, 3 and 6.

A brand as polished as what it offers.

Where it could go.

The last part of the proposal looked further ahead: turning Paula’s method into a product. I designed the concept for a tool that generates an experience report for each property, with a score and actionable recommendations, plus two routes to turn it into a business.

I also proposed a partnership model: Paula spots clients who need a better website, the studio builds it and she earns a commission on each one.

Result: a faster, compliant website with a working contact journey, a brand with a social media system and a three-phase roadmap for growth.
