LLADRÓ

CASE STUDY · VALENCIA

Why wasn’t Lladró part of my generation? That question was the starting point.

The challenge: To transform how Millennials and Gen Z perceive Lladró, not through the product but through the experience and the creation of a new community.

This project was born in October 2019, when Lladró started to become a serious option for my final degree project. What finally convinced me was a conversation with my grandmother, in which she explained the enormous meaning a Lladró ballerina had for her, a brand I didn’t know at the time.

Old but gold.

It made me wonder how a figure with such great sentimental value, which had sat on that shelf for decades, had never caught my attention, while my grandmother saw it as a symbol.

Methodology: Qualitative and quantitative research with 300+ people, mystery shopping at the Valencia boutique, interviews with 8 users, global trend analysis, competitive analysis and a full journey map of the visit to the Lladró Museum, with actions, feelings and pain points at every touchpoint. Customer segments for two profiles. Four value proposition routes developed with AEIOU, service blueprints and scenarios: Learn with Us · Created for You · Better Together · Inspire & be Inspired.

The strategy is built on two main pillars: an analysis of the brand’s current situation and context, and a study of the latest global trends, covering specific topics such as consumer habits, luxury and in-store experiences.

All with one main goal: to attract these new generations and build a Lladró community with them. A committed community, a community in motion.

STRATEGY

The deliverable is this document: all the research, analysis and service design tools, together with a final strategy proposal in the form of a development matrix.

Result: A complete service strategy with actionable proposals for physical, digital and community channels.
