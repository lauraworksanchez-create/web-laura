NATURA BOX

IDENTITY · LOGO · BRANDING

Natura Box is much more than a CrossFit box: it’s a space where community and health are the mantra.

There’s no single type of member here; young and old, men, women and all kinds of people feel welcome and motivated. The atmosphere flows with positive energy, respect and collaboration, supporting physical and mental wellbeing in every session. Natura Box combines fun, effort and health, creating an environment where everyone can push themselves and enjoy the path to a stronger, more balanced lifestyle.

NATURA BOX ✳︎

From identity to training: feel, connect and grow together.

Feel the flow. Feel alive.

In this restyling project for Natura Box, we worked to better reflect the essence of everyday life at the box: a space where health is the priority and community drives every session.

The aim of the rebrand was to create an identity every member could identify with, young or old, beginner or advanced, reinforcing the idea of a healthy mindset that flows through every action.

The new image conveys energy, inclusion and wellbeing, connecting with people not just as athletes but as part of a community that shares values, motivation and a passion for looking after themselves.
