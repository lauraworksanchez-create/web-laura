Where we are

DE LA CASA is a new ready-to-drink sangría in a square can, a nod to the classic Spanish olive oil tin. Three to start: red, white and a no/low alcohol version.

The goal is clear: show buyers real traction with preorders before Christmas, and launch in UK premium retail next summer. Waitrose, Selfridges, Whole Foods.

The challenge

Spanish at heart. Premium on the shelf.

The RTD aisle is split between loud, glossy and instagrammable, and minimal recipe-style labels. DE LA CASA needs its own place in between.

The story is already there: the family olive groves in Jaén and the restaurant in Madrid. The brand has to turn it into something people want to pick up, photograph and share.

Things I’ll keep an eye on

Four things that can slow a launch down. I’ll plan for them from day one.

01

Printing on cans

Aluminium is silver: pastels need a white base and a physical proof. A sleeve is a good option for the first run.

02

Labelling

Space reserved from day one for ABV, volume, allergens, units, pregnancy logo and barcode. UK, EU and Portman Group rules checked with a specialist.

03

The no/low version

Naming and labelling rules are different for low and alcohol-free drinks. Worth confirming early.

04

Name & trademark

Make sure De La Casa can be registered in the UK and the EU before we build the identity on it.
